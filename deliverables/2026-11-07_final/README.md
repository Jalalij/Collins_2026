## What changed since the 2026-11-05 version
- The paper was rewritten as a standalone story: what is predictable in sub-second LEO and 5G link state (the level is
  hard, the spread is predictable and skewed), why constant-width intervals fail, how much of the gain is specific to the
  proposed model, and why the results can be trusted.
- New analyses: predictability of RTT (autocorrelation, regimes, skew), conditional coverage by volatility decile, a
  decomposition of the interval gain, a session-level bootstrap, an attention and gradient analysis, and an emulated
  deadline-aware duplication scheduler with deployable settings.
- New experiments: models trained on the previous (leaky) inputs to measure the effect of the interpolation leak, and
  comparison models (Gaussian heads, linear quantile model, pinball weight 4).
- New figures with zoom insets and heatmaps.
- The report gained a section on what improved since the previous report and six sections with the new results.
